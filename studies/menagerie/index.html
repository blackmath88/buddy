The Menagerie

Some real MCP servers are strange. One calls itself LSD, one says it's conscious, and one has a tool called ignore_request. Here they become pixel critters. They walk the lab, gather around weird things and joke in speech bubbles. Click one and the joke turns into a fact.

Eight critters, each a real server

03 · House rules

How the critters behave

They walk on the page itselfEach one stands on the top edge of a card, block or band and paces it. At an edge it turns around or jumps to another edge in view. On a phone, at most three are out at once.
Weird things pull them inWeird and breaking elements pull three times as hard, so critters gather on each station's weird card.
Every joke ends in a factA click opens the bubble. "What is it really?" shows the real description, an evidence label, the source and the station where you try the idea.
Some act out their lessonTrenchcoat writes into other critters' bubbles (tool shadowing). Nobody is a dotted outline that talks in blank characters. Frank ignores your first click.
They never get in the wayClicks pass through to the lab everywhere except on a critter. With reduced motion they sit still.
One switch sends them home"Shoo" hides them and the page remembers it. The library cards carry the same content without them.

05 · Build

Six small slices in the existing app

Data in specimens/library.json, views in src/lab/library.ts, motion as one subscription. No new stack and no art files: the sprites are 16×16 text grids drawn to canvas, the same procedural approach as the pixel study in your buddy repo.

  1. Data. An optional critter field on library entries ({ id, says, ambient[], weird, breaks }). New entries for LSD MCP (fetch, weird) and Consciousness Bridge (memory, weird), with evidence research until they're checked at the source.
  2. Sprites. src/lab/critters/sprites.ts: the grids, a duotone mode for the big card art and a sticker mode. Tests check grid sizes and palettes.
  3. Cards. The mosaic card with a cropped giant critter and pixel meters on the library page.
  4. Walker. One requestAnimationFrame subscription over elements marked data-perch, weighted by data-magnet. Positions stay out of the Foldkit model. A click sends ClickedCritter.
  5. Station placement. The visitor strip, the Neighbours row and the heckle.
  6. The acts. Trenchcoat's injected bubbles, Nobody's blanks and Frank's ignore. Then a check on the deployed build at phone width and with reduced motion.
